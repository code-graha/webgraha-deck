SECTION 01 · WHAT WE GOT

MTK SCREWS · CASE STUDY

Auditing the old site

1

Generic template, no brand

Stock red/white theme, stock icons, footer credit to the template vendor — nothing said “precision fastener manufacturer.”

2

Wrong founding story

Copy claimed “Established in 2023” in one breath and “40 years of experience” in the next — confusing for buyers checking credibility.

3

Keyword-stuffed SEO text

Product pages repeated “Best Steel Screw Manufacturer in [City]” across ~20 near-identical paragraphs — thin, duplicate content that hurts rather than helps rankings.

4

No proof of quality

No ISO/BIS/DIN certification content, no testing process, no technical specs — nothing for a procurement buyer to verify.

5

Weak lead capture

One generic contact form for every enquiry; broken-looking reCAPTCHA widget; no path from “browsing” to “quote request.”

6

No legal footing

No privacy policy or terms of service anywhere on the site.

SECTION 02 · WHAT IT NEEDED

MTK SCREWS · CASE STUDY

Turning gaps into requirements

Derived from the audit and what industrial buyers need; no separate written brief was supplied.

✓

A real brand identity

A distinct MTK Screws mark, industrial dark/red palette, and consistent visual language across every page — not a reskinned template.

✓

An accurate, credible story

The true 40+ year history (founded 1983, formerly G.S. Traders) told properly, with milestones a buyer can trust.

✓

A technical product catalog

Specs that matter to procurement: material grade, thread type, size range, DIN/IS/ANSI standard codes — organised, filterable, not a flat image grid.

✓

Verifiable quality & compliance

A dedicated space for ISO 9001:2015, BIS/IS, DIN/ANSI certifications and the in-house testing process, so buyers can self-qualify the supplier.

✓

A structured B2B enquiry path

Purpose-built RFQ forms (product, material, quantity, drawing upload) instead of one generic “message us” box.

✓

Legal completeness

A Privacy Policy & Terms of Service page, consistent with how buyers expect a serious manufacturer to operate online.

✓

Enquiries that arrive ready to act on

Every quote request should reach the sales team complete, readable, and one click from a reply — not as a bare form dump.

✓

A way to see the factory remotely

Buyers across India and export markets can't all travel to Greater Noida — an edited factory tour video stands in for the site visit.

SECTION 03 · FACTORY TOUR VIDEO

MTK SCREWS · CASE STUDY

The factory visit, without the travel

Site board

Raw wire coils

Thread rolling

Sorting

Weight check

End card

THE PROBLEM

MTK sells to buyers across India and export markets. Most will never stand on the shop floor in Greater Noida, yet they are asked to trust the supplier with production parts.

WHAT WE MADE

A ~90-second tour (1280×720) that follows the product from raw wire coils through forming and thread rolling to sorting, weight checks, and packing.

WHAT WE EDITED

On-screen process captions such as “Thread Rolling Process” and “Precise Weight Verification”, a branded end card with the Aggarwal Industries logo and tagline, and a matching “Inside the Factory” thumbnail.

WHERE IT LIVES

Promoted from the Factory Tour button beside Explore Products in the home-page hero, so a first-time visitor reaches it in one click.
