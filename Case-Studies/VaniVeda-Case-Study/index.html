AN ONLINE SCHOOL WITH ONE OUTCOME

VaniVeda Language Hub is an online French-language school built around one result: the DELF, TCF and TEF scores that move candidates abroad, with Canada PR a common goal. It needed a website that could carry the whole journey from first impression to enrolment. There was no earlier site, so WebGraha built it from scratch, in one week.

SECTION 02 · WHAT IT NEEDED

VANIVEDA · CASE STUDY

04

SIX GOALS FOR THE BUILD

Derived from the audience and the delivered product; no separate written brief was supplied.

1

EXAM-FIRST CLARITY

Make DELF, TCF and TEF the first thing a visitor understands, with a way to pick the right exam.

2

PROOF OF RESULTS

Show official attestations and real candidate voices, not just claims.

3

REACH BEYOND ONE CITY

A fully remote school: show where French can take a candidate, from France and Canada to Africa.

4

CONVERT TO ENROLMENT

Clear Enrol Now paths, an exam-finder and a free-demo offer throughout the page.

5

COMFORTABLE ON A PHONE

Candidates browse and enquire on mobile, so the pages had to read well on small screens.

6

RUN ADMISSIONS BEHIND THE SCENES

Leads, student status and branded documents managed in one admin, not scattered across chats.
